## Description
DroneLink puts your drone's live camera view on your Windows PC in seconds, with nothing to configure.

Open DroneLink, copy the address it shows into DJI Fly (Transmission → Live Streaming Platforms → RTMP), and tap Start Live. The picture appears straight away. From there, show it in OBS, let people on the same Wi-Fi watch it on their phones, record it to MP4, or send it to YouTube or Facebook.

Built for drone pilots, event crews, surveyors, real estate shoots, training and anyone who needs to share what the drone sees.

• Works with DJI Fly, DJI GO 4 and DJI Pilot 2 (RTMP and RTSP)
• One address to copy, with a QR code so you don't have to type it
• Add to OBS as a Browser source that stays in place when the drone reconnects
• On-screen LIVE badge, clock and title (location, pilot), or a clean picture with one click
• Viewers on the same Wi-Fi watch in any browser, with no app to install
• Record to MP4 that stays playable even if the computer shuts down
• Go live to YouTube, Facebook or any RTMP platform without OBS
• Built-in test drone with weak-signal and signal-loss simulation, so you can rehearse without flying
• Recovers by itself when the drone drops out and reconnects

DroneLink receives the same live view your phone or controller shows. It doesn't change the drone's range and doesn't need an internet connection except for Go live. No account, no ads, no tracking.

## Notes for certification (Submission options)
DroneLink receives a live video stream from drone apps on the local network (RTMP on port 1935, preview on port 8000). No drone is needed to test it: click "Fly a test drone", then "Take off". The test picture appears in the preview, and the "Watch on other devices" link plays it in a browser. Windows may ask to allow network access the first time; choose Allow. No account or login is required.
