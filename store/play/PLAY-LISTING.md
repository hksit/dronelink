# DroneLink Mobile: Google Play listing

## App name
DroneLink Mobile

## Short description (max 80)
Live stream your DJI drone with height, direction and speed to OBS and YouTube.

## Full description
DroneLink Mobile sends your DJI drone's live camera picture and real flight data to DroneLink on your Windows or Mac computer, ready for OBS, YouTube, Facebook and big screens.

WHAT IT DOES
• Live view from the drone on your phone or tablet
• One tap "Go live": finds DroneLink on your Wi-Fi or phone hotspot by itself, or scan its QR code
• Sends height, direction, speed, distance from home, GPS and battery to DroneLink, where you choose what shows on the picture
• Reconnects by itself if the Wi-Fi or drone signal drops
• HD 720p or Full HD 1080p
• Take off, Land and Return home buttons that need a 1-second hold, so a stray tap does nothing
• Simulator mode for safe ground tests with the motors off

WORKS WITH
DJI Mini 3, Mini 3 Pro, Mini 4 Pro (with the DJI RC-N2 controller) and DJI Enterprise drones supported by the DJI Mobile SDK. Not supported: DJI RC 2 / RC screen controllers, Air 3, Avata, Mavic 3 (non-Enterprise), Mini 4K and other drones not supported by DJI's developer kit. For those, use DJI Fly with the DroneLink computer app.

YOU ALSO NEED
DroneLink on a Windows or Mac computer (free on the Microsoft Store: search "DroneLink").

SAFETY
Fly only where drones are allowed, within sight, and follow your local rules (DGCA / Digital Sky in India, CASA in Australia). Your controller's Return-to-Home and pause buttons always work. DJI's built-in safety features stay on in the drone.

This is a beta version. Feedback is welcome.

## Data safety answers
- Does the app collect or share user data? **No.** Video and flight data go directly from the phone to the user's own computer on the local network; nothing is sent to us.
- Note: the DJI Mobile SDK contacts DJI once to confirm the app (app registration) and downloads DJI's fly-safe (no-fly zone) database. Declare: **App activity / Device or other IDs: not collected by the developer**. If Google asks about third-party SDKs, list "DJI Mobile SDK (app registration and flight safety)".
- Is data encrypted in transit? Local network only (RTMP/UDP); answer "No" for encryption, as it never leaves the user's network to our servers.
- Can users request deletion? Not applicable: no account, no data stored by us.
