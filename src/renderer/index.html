Which drones work

  • DJI Fly drones (Mini, Air, Mavic 3, Avata, FPV, Neo, Flip): use the RTMP option in DJI Fly.
  • DJI GO 4 drones (Phantom 4, Mavic 2, Mavic Air): Live streaming → Custom RTMP.
  • DJI Pilot 2 (Matrice, Mavic 3 Enterprise): RTMP, or RTSP using the box under “In DJI Fly” on the right.
  • Flying with goggles only (no phone or screen controller) gives DJI Fly nothing to stream from. Use a controller with HDMI out and a capture card into OBS instead.

What you receive

The same live view the phone or controller shows, re-encoded by the drone app (usually 720p). If the drone flies far and the controller’s picture gets blocky or freezes, this picture does too. It can never be better than what the controller receives. Flight data such as altitude and GPS is not part of DJI’s live stream. Full-quality footage is still recorded on the drone’s SD card.

The picture never appears

  • The phone or controller running DJI Fly must be on the same Wi-Fi as this computer. Mobile data won’t work.
  • When Windows or macOS asks whether DroneLink may accept connections, choose Allow. On Windows, tick both Private and Public networks.
  • Some venue and guest Wi-Fi networks block devices from talking to each other. Turn on your phone’s hotspot and connect this computer to it instead. DroneLink updates the address by itself.
  • Check the address in DJI Fly matches the one on the right exactly, including /live/drone.

Check DroneLink itself

Click Fly a test drone at the top. If the test picture appears, DroneLink and OBS are working and any problem is between the drone app and this computer.

Where to find live streaming in DJI Fly

Connect to the drone, tap ··· (top right), then Transmission, Live Streaming Platforms, RTMP. Paste the address and tap Start Live. Keep DJI Fly open on screen while streaming.

In DJI Fly

  1. Connect the drone and open the camera view.
  2. Tap ···, then Transmission.
  3. Choose Live Streaming Platforms, then RTMP.
  4. Paste the address and tap Start Live.

DJI GO 4 (Phantom 4, Mavic 2): Live streaming → Custom RTMP. DJI Pilot 2: Live Streaming → RTMP.

Drone app gives you an rtsp:// address instead?

DJI Pilot 2 (Matrice, Mavic 3 Enterprise) can serve RTSP. Paste that address and DroneLink fetches it.
