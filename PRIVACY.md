# DroneLink Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

DroneLink is a desktop app that receives the live video stream from a drone app (such as DJI Fly) on your local network and shows it on your computer, in OBS, or on devices on the same Wi-Fi.

## What DroneLink collects

DroneLink does not collect, store or send any personal information to the developer. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## Where your video goes

- The drone video stays on your computer and your local network.
- Recordings are saved only to the folder you choose on your computer.
- Video leaves your network only when you use **Go live**. It is then sent directly to the platform you choose, such as YouTube or Facebook, under that platform's own privacy policy.

## What is stored on your computer

DroneLink saves your settings (for example the recording folder, on-screen title and platform choice) in its own settings file on your computer. A Go live stream key, if you enter one, is stored encrypted using your operating system's secure storage. Uninstalling DroneLink removes the app. You can delete the settings folder to remove all saved settings.

## Network access

DroneLink opens a local video receiver (ports 1935 and 8000) so your drone app and devices on the same network can connect. Anyone on the same network can view the stream page while DroneLink is running, so use it on networks you trust.

## Children

DroneLink is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
